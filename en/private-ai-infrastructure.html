FAQ

What does private or on-premise AI mean?

Models and data stay inside the organisation's own systems (own servers or a private cloud). No data is sent to third-party AI providers, and operation stays under the client's control.

Do we need special hardware?

It depends on the workload. Many use cases run on a few servers with professional-grade GPUs; the pilot measures the real requirement before anything is purchased.

How does this relate to DORA, GDPR and the EU AI Act?

Infrastructure you control makes it easier to document where data sits, keep logs and show who decided what. It does not replace legal advice: it supplies the technical evidence.

How long is a pilot and what do we get?

A pilot is a short, fixed-price project on one concrete use case, with a measured outcome you can show to management or a committee. The infrastructure stays with the client afterwards.

Are we locked in to a vendor?

No. The architecture is model-agnostic: open or commercial models can be used and swapped without rebuilding the system.
