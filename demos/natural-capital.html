05 · The decision receipt

Every claim leaves proof, not a memory.

Question, season, engines lit, coverage, outcome: the network gathers it all in a receipt and signs it with SHA-256, computed now, in your browser. Limited partners, registries and auditors verify the signature alone, trusting no one.

Chain of custody for this session

Every receipt embeds the signature of the previous one: altering a past claim would break every later one. The chain can be handed to an LP or a registry as is.
